INVESTOR CONVERSATION BRIEF · 2 OCTOBER 2026

The isolation layer
for shared AI compute.

A research-stage venture testing a focused commercial entry point: workload isolation for accelerator teams building NPU runtimes.

Research prototype Open source · MIT OR Apache-2.0

Investment thesis

Accelerator teams may need better ways to control memory access, resource interference and failure containment across workloads. Aether explores a common capability contract with named, inspectable refusal paths. The initial product hypothesis is an integration component around that boundary.

Initial buyer & offer

User: runtime and systems engineers.
Buyer: CTO or head of software.
Offer: one bounded evaluation and integration pilot, with a workload, environment, baseline, acceptance criteria and fixed end date.

Technical foundation — evidence available today

  • Rust capability and tenant models with host tests and named refusal demos.
  • QEMU boot checks for x86, RISC-V and ARM; software accelerator paths.
  • Recorded evidence bundles with commit, toolchain, lockfile and command logs.
  • Host/QEMU CI and RustSec audit passed for the maturity release.

Inspect: repository · validated CI · dependency audit · evidence index.

Why this problem matters — and what already exists

NVIDIA MIG provides hardware partitioning and isolation on supported GPUs. Linux SVA/PASID supports shared addressing and process-aware device transactions on suitable platforms. These are established mechanisms, not gaps Aether has already displaced.

Our inference: isolation is a real systems concern. Whether a reusable capability component solves an unmet problem for NPU teams must be demonstrated through discovery and a customer baseline. No performance superiority or commercial moat is established.

Proposed commercial progression

Paid evaluation→Reusable integration→Annual support

Potential paid value: supported releases, integration tooling and validated hardware support around the open-source core. Validate pricing, buyer urgency and reuse with a second customer before assuming product economics.

Open risks

No verified customers, revenue, hardware isolation or production certification are claimed. Soft SMMU is software; the partner NPU driver is a no-op stub. Runtime shims are not production plugins. No formal verification.

Security diligence: issue #161 (unchecked raw unmap helpers) is fixed in the Soft SMMU: unmap now requires a Memory+MAP capability and a foreign tenant's pin is refused with CrossTenant.
